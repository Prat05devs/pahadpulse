Pahad Pulse is an independent app. It is NOT a government app and does not represent the Government of Uttarakhand, the Government of India or any government department. It shows publicly available government and other public data, with a link to each original source.

Understand Uttarakhand through data. Pahad Pulse brings public data, alerts, maps and district-level information together in one place, so you don't have to search across different portals and reports.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Uttarakhand at a glance: key indicators, active alerts and published information on one dashboard.
• Explore all 13 districts: an interactive map with weather, alerts and key statistics per district.
• Weather and public alerts: available weather information and public alerts for different parts of the state.
• Air quality: compare available AQI, PM2.5 and PM10 readings across districts.
• Tourism and pilgrimage: published visitor figures for Haridwar, Kedarnath, Badrinath, Mussoorie, Nainital, Rishikesh and more.
• Roads, maps and connectivity: district boundaries, highways, terrain, and published mobile and broadband connectivity information.
• District comparison for business planning: pick a business type and compare two districts on the available indicators. Results depend on the availability and freshness of the underlying data. They are not a profitability forecast or an official recommendation.

OFFICIAL SOURCES OF THE INFORMATION SHOWN
• Weather and forecasts: India Meteorological Department, https://mausam.imd.gov.in
• Public alerts: National Disaster Management Authority (SACHET), https://sachet.ndma.gov.in
• Open government datasets: Open Government Data Platform India, https://data.gov.in
• Air quality (AQI, PM2.5, PM10): Central Pollution Control Board, https://cpcb.nic.in, via https://data.gov.in
• Population and demographics: Census of India, https://censusindia.gov.in
• State statistics: Directorate of Economics & Statistics, Uttarakhand, https://des.uk.gov.in
• Tourism: Uttarakhand Tourism, https://www.uttarakhandtourism.gov.in
• Pilgrim registration and tourist care: https://registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in
• Maps: © OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Every figure in the app shows its source and publication date, with a link to the original publisher. For official or time-critical decisions, always check the original source.
